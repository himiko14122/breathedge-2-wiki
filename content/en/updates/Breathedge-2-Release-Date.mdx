## Timezone Confusion and the August 30 Date

The Breathedge 2 release date of August 31, 2026 is the global target, but the Steam store page may display August 30 in certain regions. This is not a mistake, and it does not mean RedRuins moved the launch up by a day. Steam unlocks games based on a global timestamp that translates differently across timezones. When the store page shows August 30, it is simply reflecting the local date at the moment the global unlock timer reaches zero.

### Why Store Dates Vary by Region

Steam typically uses a fixed global unlock time for games that launch simultaneously worldwide. If RedRuins sets the unlock for a specific UTC hour on August 31, regions east of UTC will cross into September 1 while regions west of UTC will still be on August 30 or early August 31. The result is that Australian players might see August 31 evening or September 1, while Pacific Time players see August 31 morning. The August 30 display on some store pages is the most extreme version of this effect, appearing in regions far west of the international date line.

| Displayed Date | Region Example | Actual Unlock |
|----------------|----------------|---------------|
| August 30 | Parts of the Americas | Late August 30 local, early August 31 UTC |
| August 31 | Europe, most of Asia | August 31 local |
| September 1 | Japan, Korea, Australia | Late August 31 UTC, September 1 local |

This is standard Steam behavior and not specific to Breathedge 2. The key takeaway is that the Breathedge 2 early access release date is August 31 globally, and the August 30 store display is a timezone artifact rather than an early access window.
