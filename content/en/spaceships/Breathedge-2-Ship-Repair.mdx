### The Triage System That Works

The most effective repair routine follows a simple decision tree. When you hear a stress ping, check the affected system first. If it is life support, drop everything and fix it now. If it is hull integrity, assess whether you plan to travel in the next few minutes. If it is engine wear, postpone until you return to a safe dock. This triage prevents panic repairs and keeps your material reserve balanced.
