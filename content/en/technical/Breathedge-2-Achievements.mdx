A **combat counter achievement** requires enemy spawn rates and combat encounters to be balanced so the target number is actually reachable.

### Renderer And Launch Failures

The most common launch failure in DirectX 11 titles is a **missing or outdated GPU driver**. Players who see a black screen or an immediate crash to desktop should update their graphics driver through the manufacturer's utility rather than relying on Windows Update. The second most common issue is a **conflicting overlay**, usually from third-party tools that inject into the render pipeline.

| Issue | Likely Cause | First Fix To Try |
|-------|-------------|------------------|
| Black screen on launch | Outdated GPU driver | Update via NVIDIA/AMD app |
